Our Program

Skill Orbit Abacus Program

The Abacus Program is designed to unlock the hidden potential of your child’s mind. Using a traditional counting tool combined with modern teaching methods, children learn to visualize numbers and perform calculations mentally, enhancing both speed and accuracy.

Enroll Now!
Key Features

What Your Child Will Learn

  • Learn calculations using Upper (Heaven) and Lower (Earth) beads on the abacus
  • Strengthen memory, concentration, and logical thinking
  • Develop mental arithmetic skills for faster and accurate computation
  • Interactive and fun sessions with games, challenges, and exercises
Benefits for Children

How This Program Helps Your Child Grow

  • Improved focus and discipline
  • Enhanced problem-solving abilities
  • Strong foundation in mathematics and cognitive skills
  • Increased confidence in academics and competitive exams
Program Structure

How the Program Is Designed

  • Multiple levels from beginner to advanced
  • Step-by-step learning with practical exercises and assessments
  • Regular monitoring of progress for personalized guidance
